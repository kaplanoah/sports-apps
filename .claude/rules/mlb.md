# What MLB's app holds

- `apps/mlb/` is baseball, Worker `mlb-app`.
- The record and the live feed (`worker/src/adapters/mlb.js`):
  - The page reads each season's one record from the store, which the Worker saves as it reads MLB, as the WNBA's does.
  - The Worker reads each game being played from MLB's live feed with each update, since the schedule's copy of it runs half a minute or more behind, so every part shows the same inning, outs, and score.
  - Each update names each MLB feed that failed, with why, for the health's `feeds`. MLB has no backup feed, so there's none to probe as the WNBA's ESPN is.
- A club's sheet, which the page opens from the club's name or dot (`page/js/team-view.js` and the shared `team-numbers.js`):
  - cards of its nearest games from the slate
  - its season's runs, runs allowed, and records at home and on the road, ranked among every club's on curves
  - its postseason's as dots against the playoff field's
- A club's leaders, on its sheet (`page/js/leaders-view.js`):
  - its leaders in MLB's headline numbers, from its roster
  - AVG, OPS, and ERA count only qualified players
- A club's roster, under its sheet's Roster pill (`page/js/roster-section.js`, `roster-view.js`, `worker/src/rosters.js`, and `roster-updater.js`):
  - its active players and injured list, each with his regular season
  - The store keeps the roster for each club for the current season, reading a club's roster again only after a transaction names it, and once a day.
- A player's sheet (`page/js/player-sheet.js`, `player-view.js`, and `worker/src/players.js`):
  - A tap anywhere on his row in a roster, a box score, or a lineup, or on a starter's name in a matchup, opens it over the sheet it's in.
  - It shows, in order:
    - his facts
    - while he's on the injured list, a box with his stay and why (under "Injuries" below)
    - his postseason
    - his regular season, a hitter's ranked among MLB's ranked hitters and a starter's among its qualified starters, with what he throws
    - his last few games, from the last few weeks of his game log, read only as his games add up
  - The same job that keeps the rosters keeps his last few games for each player on a roster.
  - That job holds his season's totals, on his sheet and his club's Roster, as they were while his game log is still to read the game they count, so the two never show different games, unless that read failed.
- Injuries, in the box on a player's sheet:
  - The box's stay and why come from ESPN's injury report, read for the whole league once an hour (`worker/src/injuries.js`, with the shared `espn-adapter.js` the WNBA's reads too).
  - The box doesn't show when the box score that opened him shows him playing in a game under way or ended today, which the report hasn't caught up with (the shared `playing-now.js`).
- A game's sheet, which opens from the game's row (`page/js/game-sheet.js`, `matchup.js`, and `box-score-view.js`):
  - Its Game section shows the game:
    - its starters, on a line that slides to its Matchup
    - before first pitch, its clubs' season series (`page/js/season-series.js`)
    - its box score, or each club's lineup before first pitch
- The Runs, over the box score (`page/js/runs-view.js`):
  - A game under way has its Runs so far, and a final its Runs.
  - Each half inning that scored is under its heading, with a line for each change of the score with the score after it.
  - The runners who got on since the last change of the score are on a lighter line above the play that scored.
  - A pinch runner who scored is named with whom he ran for.
  - These come from each play in MLB's live feed.
  - While a half inning is under way, it shows who's pinch running for whom over a drawing of the field with each runner's name by his base and how he got on, and who's up against whom.
- A final's Highlights section (`worker/src/highlights.js` and `highlights-updater.js`):
  - It holds MLB's recap video, MLB.com's story, and the clip of each play, with its inning and the score after a play that scored.
  - These come from MLB's content and the plays its live feed ties each clip to.
  - The store keeps them for each of the current season's finals, read as it ends and again over the hours after, and fills past seasons incrementally after live work and reminders, newest season first. An unfilled historical Highlights section says Not available yet.
  - Current-season stills show the moment, never a headline (`worker/src/headline-stills.js`):
    - The recap's is a frame from its video.
    - A play's is MLB's own unless Claude Haiku finds a headline laid over it, then a frame from the play's video or, for a clip without frames, MLB.com's photo of the game.
    - The store asks once for each still, a few a run, since Cloudflare caps a run's requests.
    - It asks again a few times about one Claude doesn't answer, which shows as MLB's own meanwhile and then counts as clean.
  - Historical fills use MLB's own stills and ask Claude about none. Live and historical runs report separately in `highlights/status` and `highlights-history/status`.
- Detail routes forward to the store; its services fetch through adapters. Live misses share store-owned reads, and usable saved answers survive failures and restarts.
- Shared detail contracts cover highlights, pitchers, starters, rotations, club starts, rosters, player sheets, last games, and hitter rankings; see `worker.md` for validation policy.
- The box score's store (`worker/src/box-score.js`, its `adapters/box-score.js`, `game-details-updater.js`, and `watched-games.js`):
  - The store keeps the box score for every finished game of each season it keeps, the slate's first, then the rest of the current season's and each past season's, newest first.
  - It reads the box score with each update for a game a page has open.
- The Games tab (`page/js/games-view.js` and `worker/src/season-updater.js`):
  - It lists every game of the season, the All-Star Game too, under a strip of its days, each day's heading held under the strip while its games pass, and only the days near the screen drawn whole.
  - Each game's row (`page/js/games-view.js`):
    - each game away from today shows its starter with his arm
    - each club's record, as of the game once it's been played, from the schedule, and its record now for a game still to come, from the standings, as today's rows show it
    - each postseason game, its series as it stood at the game, or its game until both clubs are known (`page/js/series-games.js`)
    - If necessary under one MLB may not need
    - each final, its inning when it didn't end in the ninth
  - The list comes from the season's whole schedule, which the store keeps a month to a document in `schedules-<year>`.
  - The store reads MLB's whole schedule once a day and the days around today with each update, and saves only the months that changed, removing a month of playoff games that lost every game.
  - A playoff game is listed only while MLB's postseason or the days around today list it, since MLB drops one a series no longer needs from those first.
  - A whole schedule or postseason MLB answers with no games drops no game, and a whole schedule with none saves nothing.
  - Each game has its ballpark, and a game away from its home club's own one says where in its label.
- The slate (`page/js/slate.js`):
  - The slate, each club's last, today's, and next games kept apart from the season's whole schedule, is what the header, the Updates box, and the store follow.
  - The store also follows the starts of games MLB put off or called off, kept on the snapshot from the schedule's days around today, since a rainout's make-up is often set after midnight.
  - Each device keeps the slate's ranking for itself, in name order until its first drag (`page/js/kept-on-device.js`).
  - Each device also keeps when its Updates box counts from, its first look or last dismissal (`page/js/kept-on-device.js`).
  - Only phones and tablets show the Updates box (`page/js/updates.js`).
- Starting pitchers, which the Worker also serves:
  - each starting pitcher's matchup numbers, ranked among MLB's qualified starters (`worker/src/pitchers.js`)
  - for a club yet to name one, its last starters and their rest (`worker/src/rotations.js`)
  - The store keeps both for the current season, with the qualified starters of each season it keeps from before, reading MLB as a game ends and only the pitchers who pitched in it, so a sheet reads only the store (`worker/src/pitcher-updater.js`).
- Past seasons and their standings:
  - The Worker fills in each past season's record once, with its standings, from MLB (`worker/src/past-seasons.js`).
- Search, a round button beside the tab bar:
  - It finds the season's games as the WNBA's does (`page/js/search-terms.js`), from:
    - MLB's names for its clubs, ballparks, leagues and divisions
    - each league's own rounds
    - its starters, by the games each is named to start
  - It finds the app's own pages as the WNBA's does (`page/js/search-pages.js`):
    - each club's sheet and its Roster
    - each player on a club's roster this season, by the names MLB uses for him, from the list of players the store keeps beside its rosters (`worker/src/player-index.js`)
    - the latest final's Highlights and the next game's Matchup
    - the bracket and the News tab
    - the standings' playoff fields and each league's divisions
    - settings and each setting
- The News tab (`worker/src/news-feeds.js`, `news-prompts.js`, and `news-updater.js`, and `page/js/news-league.js`):
  - It shows the news as the WNBA's does, from MLB's outlets and the Mets' own.
  - The same shared news job keeps it by MLB's prompt: anything about the Mets, and another club's news only when it matters across baseball.
  - It has a settings switch for each of its paid outlets.

- Schedule publications keep a small `schedule-revisions/<year>` document. History completion is tied to that revision; an interrupted publication remains incomplete until repaired.
